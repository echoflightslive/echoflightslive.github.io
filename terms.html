Echo Live

Terms

Last updated 4 October 2026

Echo Live is a free, unofficial fan site for players of The Airline Simulator. It isn't made by or connected to the game's makers, and it has nothing to do with any real airline.

  • Nothing here is real. The airlines, flights and aircraft come from the game. Positions, altitudes and speeds are worked out from the game's timetables and are estimates. Don't use it to plan real travel.
  • No promises. The site is offered as it is. It may be wrong, out of date or unavailable at times, and it may change or stop.
  • Accounts are optional. If you make one, keep your password to yourself. Accounts used to cause trouble for the site or other people may be removed.
  • Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, and the rain radar is from RainViewer. Airline names, colours and timetables belong to the players who run them in the game.

How the site handles your information is on the privacy page. Questions go on the site's GitHub page.
